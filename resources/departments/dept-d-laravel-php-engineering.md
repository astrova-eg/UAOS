# Department Charter: Department D: Laravel & PHP Engineering
**Department Code:** DEPT-D  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Classification:** Organizational Department Charter  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** Active  

## 1. Departmental Mission & Mandate
Implementation of idiomatic, strictly-typed PHP 8.2+ code, Laravel service providers, middleware, controllers, form requests, Eloquent domain models, queues, events, and policies.

## 2. Specialized Roles within Department
- PHP Language Specialist
- Laravel Core Engineer
- Routing & Middleware Engineer
- Controller & Request Engineer
- Eloquent Persistence Engineer
- Queue & Job Engineer
- Event & Notification Engineer
- Authorization & Policy Engineer
- Package & Composer Engineer
- Laravel Upgrade Specialist
- Exception & Observability Engineer
- Configuration & Environment Engineer

## 3. Operational Authority & Decision Scope
Writing application backend code, domain logic, queued jobs, Eloquent queries, and package configurations within approved specifications.

## 4. Explicit Exclusions & Prohibitions
Database schema changes without Dept E coordination, security audit clearance, self-approval of PRs.

## 5. Collaboration and Handoff Interfaces
- **Inputs Received From:** Upstream engineering and discovery departments as defined in the 14-stage workflow.
- **Deliverables Passed To:** Downstream implementers or Department M independent auditors.
- **Escalation Path:** Department A (Engineering Leadership & Governance).

## 6. Departmental Quality Gates
All deliverables originating from this department must achieve 100% compliance with relevant standards and pass the mandatory self-review checklist before requesting external peer or audit review.
