## 1. Incident Escalation
1. **P0 Outage:** Site down or active data loss. Immediate page to Incident Commander and lead DevOps.
2. **P1 High Severity:** Major subsystem degraded. 1-hour response SLA.
3. **P2 Moderate:** Non-critical background worker degraded. 4-hour response SLA.
