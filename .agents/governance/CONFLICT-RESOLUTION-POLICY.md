# Conflict Resolution Policy
**Document ID:** GOV-DOC-019  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Classification:** Dispute Resolution Protocol  

## 1. Resolution Hierarchy
When two agents disagree on an architectural approach, library choice, or implementation strategy:
1. **Level 1:** Technical debate moderated by `conflict-resolution-specialist` (CONF-001) evaluating empirical benchmarks and Project Profile constraints.
2. **Level 2:** If unresolved, escalated to `chief-software-architect` (ARCH-001) for architectural rulings or `chief-engineering-officer` (CHENG-001) for strategic rulings.
3. Rulings are recorded in an official ADR and become binding precedent.
